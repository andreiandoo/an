Frequently asked questions

What share of the company would you take?

If you pay nothing for development, we split equally, 50/50. If you pay part of the cost, my share is smaller and we set it after we understand the idea. Everything goes in a contract, and each of us earns our share gradually, over a few years, so it stays fair if one of us steps back.

Why do you not work just for money?

Because I want the same interest as you: for the product to work, not for the project to end. If you only want an app delivered, that is what my services are for.

How much do I pay with the reduced price?

From €3,000 for web and €5,000 for mobile, the same starting prices as my services, paid in stages at a fixed price. I cover the rest of the cost in exchange for my share of the company. I invoice through TIXELLO SRL.

Why does someone else need to have the problem, besides me?

Because I have made this mistake myself: I built starting from just an idea. Before the first line of code I want to hear the problem from someone who has it. And when the first version is ready, we need to know clearly who we show it to. If you are the only one with the problem, it is too early to build.

How many founders do you work with at the same time?

At most 2, besides Tixello, so each one gets my time and fast answers.

Do I need to have a company already?

Not to get started. We set it up when we write the terms, and the code and accounts move to it.
